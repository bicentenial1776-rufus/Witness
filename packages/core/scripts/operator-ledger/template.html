What is logged

  • login — a real sign-in (password, Apple, magic link). A restored session on app open is not one.
  • gedcom_import_completed / _failed — file bytes, people, families, places, provider.
  • Every AI call's model and tokens: biographies and context, story arcs, tree syntheses, research briefs, headstone reads, media readings.

How to read it

  • Sign-in dates for existing accounts come from Supabase Auth's last sign-in; the login event adds a full history from 7 September 2026 on.
  • Cost is an estimate at list rates per million tokens: Sonnet 4.6 at $3 in / $15 out, Opus 5 at $15 in / $75 out. Media readings before 7 September have no token figures.
  • Accounts tagged test are Rufus's own test sign-ups and are left out of the headline counts.
