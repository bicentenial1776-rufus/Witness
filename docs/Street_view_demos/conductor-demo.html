The Conductor

A score composed live, bar by bar — functional harmony, a family leitmotif, voice-led strings, horns, harp, timpani, choir and bells. Nothing loops; every phrase is written the moment before you hear it.
 
stillness majesty
The same engine scores every night of the Lantern games. Cadence is what a finished chapter sounds like: predominant, dominant, home — and where the mode is dark, the arrival opens into major light (a Picardy third), so even Salem ends in grace.
