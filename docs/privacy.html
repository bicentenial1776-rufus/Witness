Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

The short version: your original family file is encrypted on your device before it reaches us — we store it, but we can't open it. Witness keeps only the minimal index needed to answer your questions, and your living relatives are excluded from all AI processing, sharing, and matching.

Who we are

Witness — Family History Intelligence ("Witness," "we") is an independent iOS and iPad app operated by Rufus Howe. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@witnesslives.com.

What we collect and why

Account information

Your email address and authentication credentials, used to create and secure your account, and your subscription status, used to manage your trial and membership.

Your family tree

When you import a GEDCOM file, two things happen:

  • Your original file is encrypted on your device before upload and stored as an unreadable blob. We cannot open or read it. It exists so you can restore your import.
  • A minimal index of names, dates, places, and family relationships is stored in our database so the app can answer your questions and generate historical context. This index is protected by access controls scoped to your account alone.

Living relatives

People in your tree who appear to be living are treated differently: they are excluded from all AI processing, never appear in shareable content, and are stored with an additional layer of encryption. They never leave your device unprotected.

Location

Field features like "I'm Here" mode and cemetery search use your device's GPS to match nearby places against your own tree. Your location is used on your device at the moment you use these features. We do not track, log, or store your movements, and location is never shared with third parties.

Usage and diagnostics

We collect basic diagnostic information (crashes, performance) to keep the app working. We do not sell data, run ads, or use third-party advertising trackers.

AI-generated content

Witness uses Anthropic's Claude models to generate ancestor biographies, historical context, digest notes, and research briefs. To do this, relevant details from your tree's index — names, dates, places, and family structure of deceased individuals only — are sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. Generated results are cached so repeat requests don't require reprocessing.

Sharing

Discovery cards and research briefs are shared only when you choose to share them, using the standard iOS share sheet. Living persons are never included in shareable content. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete any imported tree from within the app, which removes its index and stored file. To delete your account and all associated data, email support@witnesslives.com from your account address; we will confirm removal. Cached AI content tied to your trees is deleted with them.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Your original GEDCOM file is encrypted on your device before upload with keys we do not hold. Database access is restricted by row-level security so that your tree is queryable only by your authenticated account.

Children

Witness is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Deceased ancestors in your tree are historical records, not app users.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Witness supports all of these regardless of jurisdiction — email support@witnesslives.com and we will honor your request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will note the new effective date here and inform you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
