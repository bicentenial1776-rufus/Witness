Witness, as it stands: one subject, four verbs, two loops

Two days ago the feature map showed six families of screens funneling into a room with one door. This is the after picture: what each feature is now, what feeds it, and the two loops — the reading week and the research round trip — that make them one system.

8 August 2026 · drawn from main at 978a904 · live on phone, native iPad ≥900pt, and app.witnesslives.com

01The shape

Four tabs, four verbs: Home reads, Tree works, Explore asks, Map goes. The fifth slot is empty on purpose — Street View is its claimant. Every tab, and every desk on the front page, ultimately lands on the same room — the Portrait — but the room is now a junction, not a terminus: it can send you onward to a place, a household, a chain of descent, an era, a finding, or the next piece of the week's issue.

The funnel became a wheel. Everything still meets at the Portrait — but now it's the hub the spokes pass through, not the drain they empty into.

02The two loops

The system runs on two cycles at different speeds. The reading loop turns weekly and asks nothing of the user but twenty minutes. The research loop turns on the user's schedule — months, sometimes — and is the only path by which Witness's findings become part of the permanent family record.

04Deliberately absent

  • The fifth tab. Empty until Street View earns it. Filling a slot because it's free is the opportunism this year's work corrected.
  • The cousin network. Deferred again, on purpose (Rufus, 8 Aug): a network-effect feature with no network yet fires never and looks broken. Kindred couples is its single-tree rehearsal; the society campaign is its future seedbed.
  • "I'm Here" notifications. The lock-screen nudge in ancestral territory — the true field entry the Nearby tab was standing in for. Future work by choice, not omission.
  • The Lens. Branch focus lives on as a future filter on the ledger and the Issue ("an issue about the Acadian line"), never as the spine.
  • A tree diagram. Still refused. Orientation is carried by one-dimensional instruments — path, compass, running head — because the moment anything fans out in two dimensions, it's a worse Ancestry.
