# You built the tree. Now read it.

*WitnessLives — one page. September 2026. Designed version: published as an
artifact from the 2026-09-08 session; this file is the text of record.*

Tree-building software is superb at finding facts and proving them. Witness is
built for reading what was found. It opens the GEDCOM your builder exports,
never edits it, and turns a well-sourced tree into something the whole family
can sit with each morning. Neither job is complete without the other.

## Two jobs, both done well

Ancestry, FamilySearch, Family Tree Maker, RootsMagic: each is excellent at the
first, and Witness would fall very short without them. Witness does only the
second, and does it for the whole family.

| | Tree building | Tree reading |
|---|---|---|
| The job | **Find the facts and prove them.** Records, hints, citations, conflicts settled. | **Read what the facts say.** A person in their moment, in the words of the record. |
| Excellent at | Searching billions of records. A hint engine that never sleeps. The discipline of sourcing. The chart that shows the whole. | Turning a proven line into a story. Noticing what only a full tree makes visible. Giving every reader a way in. |
| A session ends with | A better-proven tree: a new person, a new citation, a conflict resolved. | One line of the family understood, and **one specific record** worth going back for. |
| Who it serves | **The researcher**, whose craft this is, and who holds the tree for everyone. | **The whole family**, up to five people, each reading from their own place in it. |
| What it needs from the other | Someone to read what has been found, and a reason to go find the next record. | **Excellent fact-finding.** Without a well-sourced tree, Witness has nothing worth reading. |

## The gap: most people who want the story have never had a way into the research

**10 : 1** — story-buyers to researchers. Roughly twenty-seven million
AncestryDNA kits have been sold against about three million paying research
subscribers. Nine of every ten want the story; one does the work that makes
the story true.

In nearly every family that one is the kinkeeper, who holds a tree the rest of
the family never opens, because the tools were built for the builder. That is
not a failing of the tools. It is a room that was never built beside them. Tree
software serves the one superbly. Witness is the reading room for the nine, and
it is only as good as the tree the one has proven.

## The gain: together they close a loop neither can close alone

Three verbs describe everything Witness does. Each one ends by handing the
reader back to the builder with a better question than they started with, and
the builder answers it with a record.

**Experience** — the tree as story, with the record as the authority and the
AI's contribution labeled plainly. A daily edition: one line of the family,
founder to reader. A Portrait for every person: life, times, family, sources.
Who was alive for the Mayflower landing or the 1918 flu. Near Me: whose history
is within a mile of where you stand.

**Notice** — matches and checks that arrive as questions, never verdicts. You
judge every one. Tree Health: twenty-two data-integrity checks over every person, family, and date.
Colonial passenger lists: 105 voyages, 6,296 passengers. The Acadian rolls; the
Union regiments of the Civil War. At the Stone: the camera reads a headstone
against your tree.

**Refine** — every finding ends the same way: back to the builder, where the
real work of proving it is done. A structured Research Brief for each brick wall.
Ancestry and FamilySearch open pre-filled for the person you are reading. A
confirmed crossing becomes an immigration event in your Witness copy.
Re-export from your builder and read again.

build → read → notice → a better question → build

## Where the line sits

- **Not a tree builder.** It has no editing surface for your source tree and does not want one. Family Tree Maker, Ancestry, RootsMagic, and FamilySearch already do that job well; Witness sits beside them.
- **Not a source of truth.** A match is a question with its reasons shown. What you confirm is kept in your Witness copy alone; the tree where it lives is never touched.
- **Nothing without the research.** Research discovers families through the record; that craft is the foundation. Witness helps people enjoy what research found, and sends them back to it with better questions.
