# You built the tree. Now read it.

*WitnessLives — one page. September 2026. Designed version: published as an
artifact from the 2026-09-08 session; this file is the text of record.*

Every family-tree program is built for accumulating facts. None of them is
built for reading what the facts say. Witness is the reading companion for the
tree you already have: it opens your GEDCOM, never edits it, and turns it into
something the whole family can sit with each morning.

## Two different jobs

Ancestry, FamilySearch, Family Tree Maker, RootsMagic: each one is excellent at
the first job. Witness does only the second.

| | Tree building | Tree reading |
|---|---|---|
| The unit of work | A fact. Add a person, attach a record, clear a hint. | A **person**, in their moment, in the words of the record. |
| A session ends with | A bigger tree and a longer to-do list. Five thousand names, one of them memorized. | One line of the family understood, and **one specific record** worth going back for. |
| Errors | Compound silently. A wrapped date or a merged cousin sits unnoticed for years. | Are **noticed** and named, one tap from the person they concern. |
| Who can use it | The researcher. The rest of the family gets a chart nobody can read. | **Up to five people**, each reading from their own place in the family. |
| When the research stops | The tree goes dark. The subscription lapses; the file sits on a laptop. | The tree **keeps talking**. A fresh edition every morning, from what is already there. |

## The gap: most people who want the story will never do the research

**10 : 1** — story-buyers to researchers. Roughly twenty-seven million
AncestryDNA kits have been sold against about three million paying research
subscribers. The other nine bought a story and were handed a database.

In nearly every family there is one person, the kinkeeper, who holds the tree.
Everyone else is interested in their heritage and uninterested in the work of
building it. Tree software serves the one. Witness is for the nine.

## The gain: reading closes a loop that building alone cannot

Three verbs describe everything Witness does. Each one hands the reader back to
the tree with a better question than they started with.

**Experience** — the tree as story, with the record as the authority and the
AI's contribution labeled plainly. A daily edition: one line of the family,
founder to reader. A Portrait for every person: life, times, family, sources.
Who was alive for the Mayflower landing or the 1918 flu. Near Me: whose history
is within a mile of where you stand.

**Notice** — matches and checks that arrive as questions, never verdicts. You
judge every one. Twenty-two data-integrity checks, the FTAnalyzer catalog.
Colonial passenger lists: 105 voyages, 6,296 passengers. The Acadian rolls; the
Union regiments of the Civil War. At the Stone: the camera reads a headstone
against your tree.

**Refine** — every finding ends the same way: back to the builder with a
specific record to find. A structured Research Brief for each brick wall.
Ancestry and FamilySearch open pre-filled for the person you are reading. A
confirmed crossing becomes an immigration event in your Witness copy.
Re-export from your builder and read again.

build → read → notice → a better question → build

## What Witness is not

- **Not a tree builder.** It has no editing surface for your source tree and does not want one. It sits beside Family Tree Maker, Ancestry, and FamilySearch, not in front of them.
- **Not a source of truth.** A match is a question with its reasons shown. What you confirm is kept in your Witness copy alone; the tree where it lives is never touched.
- **Not a replacement for research.** Research discovers families through the record. Witness helps people enjoy what research found, and sends them back to it with better questions.
