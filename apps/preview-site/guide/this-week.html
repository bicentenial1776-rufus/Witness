The Screens

This Week in Your Family.

The weekly digest: the anniversaries — births and deaths — that fall in the next seven days, with one life featured and placed in its historical moment.

How you get here: from the Home tab’s This Week card, or from the Sunday morning notification. The week runs from today through the next seven days.

1 2 3 4 5 6
  1. The week’s span, and — when the week is crowded — how many anniversaries the digest chose from. Only the best few make the cut.
  2. ✦ FEATURED — the week’s lead life, chosen from the people your “Who gets featured” scope covers. The day it falls on is spelled out.
  3. The name, its lineage mark, and your relationship — who this is and how they connect to you. The ⇅ here says Ardelia is on your direct line.
  4. The parentage line — whose child they were.
  5. The anniversary itself — born or died, how many years ago, and where.
  6. The note — a few AI-written sentences placing the person in their historical moment. Generated once, then kept; “Placing them in their moment…” shows while it’s being written.

Below the note, “Their full story ›” opens the person’s page. The rest of the week’s anniversaries follow as plain one-line cards — same information, no ceremony, every card tappable. A long featured story simply pushes them below the fold, as here.

A quiet week is honest: “no dated anniversaries in your tree fall in the next seven days” means exactly that. And people outside your featuring scope still appear in their families’ screens — they just don’t lead the digest.

The Sunday reminder

On the You tab, Weekly reminder arms a Sunday-morning notification carrying the week’s top anniversary. Visiting This Week re-arms next week’s with fresh content.
