The Screens

Home — this week’s issue.

Home is a weekly edition. Each Monday a new issue is set — the same one on every device you own — and each part of Witness files exactly one piece into it: a lead life, a single curiosity from the Tree Check, the Archives’ waiting count, one pattern your family made. Nothing here demands action — it’s a paper to read with coffee, and a thin week simply prints fewer pieces.

How you get here: the Home tab — the app opens here. The gear in the top corner opens You (account, trees, and preferences).

Awaiting screenshot: the Home tab as this week’s issue — the NO. dateline, THE LEAD card, and at least one desk (FROM THE TREE CHECK or FROM THE ARCHIVES) in frame. Target: shots/home-issue.png.
  1. The dateline — NO. 32 · THE WEEK OF AUGUST 3. The issue number is the week of the year; the edition holds until Monday.
  2. THE LEAD — the week’s featured life, with years, place, and how this person connects to you. The same person leads This Week; here they get the front page.
  3. The note — the AI-written passage placing them in their historical moment, drawn from the same cache the digest uses. THEIR FULL STORY › opens their person page.
  4. FROM THE TREE CHECK — one curiosity, chosen for the week. Tapping the sentence opens the person it names; the small line beneath carries the open count and the line holding most of them, and opens the full workbench. “A prompt, not a problem” is the intended temperature.
  5. FROM THE ARCHIVES — how many federal records await your judgment, when any do. THE PATTERN — one ocean crossing or migration, told as a sentence; it lands on the person or the move itself, and MORE PATTERNS › opens the rest.
  6. The tabs — Home, Tree (the Register & Family Graph), Explore, and Map (which carries Near me). Four, on purpose.

Below the desks, the standing furniture: ON THIS DAY (a family anniversary falling today — or, failing that, a historical event with how many of your people were alive for it) with the THIS WEEK IN YOUR FAMILY › link beneath; YOUR TREE, a one-line stat strip (people · families · generations) that opens the Tree tab; PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF, which remembers the last person or screen you were reading; and the EXPLORE shelf — a horizontal row of event cards, each with its year and a live “N ALIVE” count, ending in THE LIBRARY ›.

Once you tap into a piece, the issue follows you: the person page carries a slim band — NO. 32 · NEXT: THE PATTERN › — so the next piece is one tap, however deep the reading has wandered. Dismiss it with the × and it stays quiet for the sitting.

A brand-new tree’s first issues run thin, and the front page says so — the lead’s note is written by a daily job, archive matches by a scheduled worker, map pins by the geocoder. Richer issues arrive as the presses warm over the first days.
